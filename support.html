PraiseU Support

One tap to celebrate the hands-on work you do

Need help or have an idea? Open a support request on GitHub. A free GitHub account is needed.

Getting started

After you finish a job, hand your iPhone to the customer. They tap awesome, and you both get a quick celebration. Then PraiseU asks for their name, and they just say it. They can tap Skip to stay anonymous. Tap Private stats to see today's, this week's, this month's and all-time counts, plus who signed.

Tip: tap awesome once yourself before your first visit, so iOS asks for microphone permission while you're holding the phone. You can delete that test record in Private stats.

Questions

It shows a text box instead of listening

Voice signing needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, running iOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. iOS may also need a few minutes after first launch to download its speech model. Until then, visitors can tap the microphone key on the keyboard and say their name.

It heard the name wrong

The visitor can tap Not right? Try again before it saves. You can also swipe the record away in Private stats.

Can a customer see my stats?

No. Private stats need your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. They also close automatically when you leave the app.

Why doesn't the screen turn off?

PraiseU keeps the screen awake while it's open, so the phone doesn't lock mid-visit. Press the side button to lock it. To keep visitors inside the app, turn on Guided Access in Settings › Accessibility.

No sound?

The fanfare follows your silent switch. Turn silent mode off to hear it.

How do I export or delete my history?

Open Private stats. Export CSV shares a spreadsheet of times and names. Swipe a row to delete it, or use Delete all history.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
