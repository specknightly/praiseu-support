PraiseU Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

In short: PraiseU keeps everything on your iPhone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the app never sends your data to us or anyone else. Entropy Shield, the developer, receives none of your data.

What PraiseU stores

Each time someone taps awesome, PraiseU saves the date and time. If the visitor chooses to sign, it also saves the name they said or typed. That's all. These records are stored only in the app's private storage on your iPhone.

Microphone and speech

The microphone is used only after someone taps sign your name, for at most 8 seconds. Speech is turned into text on your iPhone by Apple's Speech framework, and the name is picked out by Apple Intelligence's on-device model. Audio is never saved and never leaves the device. PraiseU doesn't use cloud AI. iOS may download Apple's on-device speech model once; Apple handles that download.

Face ID

Private stats are protected with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. iOS handles authentication. PraiseU never receives your biometric data.

Exporting

You can export your history as a CSV file. This only happens when you tap Export CSV, and the file goes only where you send it with the iOS share sheet.

Backups

Like most apps, PraiseU's data may be included in iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, depending on your device settings. Apple's privacy policy covers those backups.

Deleting data

In Private stats, swipe a record to delete it, or tap Delete all history. Deleting the app also removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Your customers' names

Visitors choose whether to sign, and they say or type their own name. If you use PraiseU with customers, please let them know their name is saved on your phone, and delete any record when asked.

Children

PraiseU is a professional tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
